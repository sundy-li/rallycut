A SMALLER EDITING ROUTINE

Play. Pick.
Share.

Start with a short match clip.
Get from footage to highlights in three steps.

  1. 01 / FIND

    Bring your match.

    Import local videos and run analysis. Candidate rallies appear as the video is processed.

  2. 02 / REVIEW

    Keep your best moments.

    Replay with ball tracks. Adjust the boundaries, add a missed rally, or remove a false one. Undo and redo are there when you need them.

  3. 03 / EXPORT

    Take the tracks with you.

    Select your rallies. In the export bar, enable ball trajectories and rally numbers. Export an MP4 with sound — one rally or a whole reel.

Before your first match

  • Install Python 3.10+ (3.12 recommended), FFmpeg and FFprobe separately. Windows also needs WebView2.
  • First setup needs internet. After setup, analysis and export happen on your computer.
  • The macOS app is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized. Windows is not publisher-signed.
  • Detection needs your review. Intel Mac and Windows need further real-device testing.
Model and privacy notes

Weights are not bundled. Upstream weight licensing is still under review. The app does not automatically upload footage or send telemetry. Hosting and first-run download services may keep their own access logs.
